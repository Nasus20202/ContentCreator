# Series: Wrong About Space

**id:** `wrong-about-space` · **format:** vertical short, 35–50 s · **cadence:** weekly (Sunday, unless Sky Tonight takes the slot) · **research:** sourced/computed

## Promise to the viewer

> "Something almost everyone believes about space — and what's actually true, with the evidence."

## Myth selection

- A **widely held** belief (dark side of the Moon, seasons from distance to the Sun, the Sun is yellow, no gravity on the ISS).
- The correction must be **demonstrable** with our tools: computed geometry (`orbit3d`, `skyView`), a real image, or one sourced number.
- Never mock the viewer; the tone is "here's the cool part".

## Episode structure

| Beat     | Seconds  | Template                         | Purpose                              |
| -------- | -------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Hook     | 0–4      | `image` / `factCard` + `title`   | State the myth, flag it as wrong     |
| Why      | 4–20     | any                              | Why people believe it (fair version) |
| Evidence | 20–40    | `orbit3d` / `image` / `factCard` | What's true, shown                   |
| Outro    | last 4–5 | `outro`                          | Follow ask                           |

## Writing rules

- The title states the truth, not just the myth (no hook that leaves people believing the myth).
- One myth per episode.

## Hashtags baseline

`#space #astronomy #science #mythbusting` + 1 topic tag.
