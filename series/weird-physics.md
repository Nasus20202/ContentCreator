# Series: Weird Physics

**id:** `weird-physics` · **format:** vertical short, 40–55 s · **cadence:** weekly (Thursday) · **research:** computed (formula + inputs via `cc fact add --computed`) or sourced

## Promise to the viewer

> "One physics result that sounds wrong but isn't — worked out with real numbers."

## Topic selection

- The result must be **counter-intuitive and exact**: fall through a tunnel through Earth → 42 minutes, whatever the tunnel's angle; a clock on a mountain runs faster; you weigh less at the equator.
- The number must be computable from a stated formula with sourced inputs (constants from NIST/CODATA, planetary data from NASA fact sheets). Record method + inputs.
- Say the idealisation out loud ("ignoring air and heat") when one is used.

## Episode structure

| Beat   | Seconds  | Template                       | Purpose                             |
| ------ | -------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Hook   | 0–4      | `factCard` / `image` + `title` | The surprising claim as a statement |
| Setup  | 4–15     | `image` / `journey`            | The situation, concretely           |
| Why    | 15–38    | `journey` / `compare` / `laps` | The physics, one step at a time     |
| Payoff | 38–48    | `factCard`                     | The number, and what it means       |
| Outro  | last 4–5 | `outro`                        | Follow ask                          |

## Writing rules

- No equations on screen; one "because" per episode.
- Every computed number has a fact with `--computed --method --inputs`.
