# Series: One Shot

**id:** `one-shot` · **format:** vertical short, 30–45 s · **cadence:** weekly (Saturday) · **research:** sourced (the image's own release page)

## Promise to the viewer

> "One real space photo, and the one detail in it you would have missed."

## Image selection

- A **real** image from NASA/ESA/JWST/Hubble/a mission camera, with its release page as the source. Never AI, never an artist's concept.
- It must hide something worth zooming into: a planet as a dot, a galaxy behind a galaxy, a human-made object, a scale surprise.
- Credit on screen (automatic) and in the post.

## Episode structure

| Beat  | Seconds  | Template                | Purpose                       |
| ----- | -------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Hook  | 0–4      | `image` + `title`       | The whole picture + the claim |
| Look  | 4–20     | `image` (zoom + marker) | Walk the eye to the detail    |
| Scale | 20–35    | `factCard` / `compare`  | The number that makes it land |
| Outro | last 4–5 | `outro`                 | Follow ask                    |

## Writing rules

- Say what took the picture and when.
- If colours are processed or wavelengths are shifted (infrared), say so in one clause.

## Hashtags baseline

`#space #astronomy #nasa #astrophotography` + 1 topic tag.
