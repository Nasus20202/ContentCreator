# Series: Mission Control

**id:** `mission-control` · **format:** vertical short, 40–55 s · **cadence:** weekly (Wednesday) + same-day for dated milestones · **research:** sourced, primary sources only

## Promise to the viewer

> "What's happening in space exploration right now — and why it matters — straight from the mission's own sources."

## Story selection

- A **concrete, dated** event: a launch, a landing, a flyby, a milestone (Voyager 1 at one light-day), a first image, a crew mission (Artemis).
- Prefer stories where we can show **real mission imagery** and one number that makes the scale click.
- Sources: the agency's own release (NASA, ESA, JAXA, ISRO, CNSA), mission page, or the paper. No aggregators, no rumours, no "reportedly".
- Skip stories whose outcome is pending at publish time unless the script says so explicitly ("launch targeted for…").

## Episode structure

| Beat    | Seconds  | Template                      | Purpose                              |
| ------- | -------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Hook    | 0–4      | `image` + `title`             | What happened, in one sentence       |
| Context | 4–20     | `image` / `journey` / `scale` | Where/how far/how fast, to scale     |
| Why     | 20–40    | `factCard` / `compare`        | The one number or first that matters |
| Next    | 40–48    | `image` / `factCard`          | What happens next (dated)            |
| Outro   | last 4–5 | `outro`                       | Follow ask                           |

## Writing rules

- Tense must be right **at publish time**; record the publish-time assumption in the fact-check notes.
- Name the agency and mission once; no acronyms without expansion.
- Never speculate about cause of failures; quote the agency's statement or say it is under investigation.

## Hashtags baseline

`#space #nasa #spaceexploration #science` + mission tag (`#artemis`, `#voyager`).
