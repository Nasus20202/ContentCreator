## 2026-11-11 · infra · claude

## 2026-11-11 · strategy · claude

**Launch with one series: 'Sky This Week' — weekly, location-specific, computed night-sky guide (vertical short, 40–55 s)**

Best fit of (a) defensible quality: every claim is computed from ephemerides, so we can't drift into slop, (b) recurring demand with natural weekly novelty, (c) monetisation beyond Shorts ads (gear/sponsor fit: Astronomy.com's weekly sky column is Celestron-sponsored), (d) low risk under YouTube's July-2026 'inauthentic content' clarification, which targets repetitive mass-produced content rather than AI-assisted work with original input. Shorts RPM is small for any niche (estimates /usr/bin/zsh.04–1.44 for education), so Shorts are treated as a funnel; long-form and sponsors are the revenue path. Review after 8 published episodes using recorded metrics.

Alternatives considered:
- General space-facts shorts (rejected: most saturated slop niche, no data moat)
- Science/tech news shorts (deferred: needs sourced-facts workflow + speed; good second series once the pipeline is proven)
- Long-form explainers first (deferred: higher RPM but far more production per video; reuse templates later)
