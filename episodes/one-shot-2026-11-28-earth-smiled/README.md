### Post copy

**Find Earth in this photo of Saturn 🪐🔵**

On 19 July 2013, NASA's Cassini slipped into Saturn's shadow, so the Sun was hidden behind the planet and the rings lit up from behind. Then it looked back. Under the rings, 1.44 billion km away, is a tiny blue dot: Earth, with the Moon a faint bump beside it. It was the first time people on Earth knew in advance that their picture would be taken from that far away.
