### Post copy

**Two NASA probes just slingshotted past Earth toward Mars 🔴**

NASA's twin ESCAPADE spacecraft launched in November 2025, when Mars was on the far side of the Sun. Instead of waiting on the ground, they spent a year looping around a point about 1.6 million km from Earth, then came back. On 7 and 9 November 2026 each one swept past Earth (the second at about 1,000 km) and fired its engine for about 9 minutes to head for Mars. Arrival is planned for 7 and 9 September 2027, when they'll fly in formation to study how the solar wind strips Mars's atmosphere.

Source:
