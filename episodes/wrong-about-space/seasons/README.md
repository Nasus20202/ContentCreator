### Post copy

**Winter isn't caused by distance from the Sun 🌍☀️**

Earth is closest to the Sun in early January: about 147.1 million km on 3 January 2027, compared with 152.1 million km on 5 July, only about 3% apart. Seasons come from the tilt of Earth's axis (23.44°). In central Europe the noon Sun stands about 17° high on 21 December and about 63° high on 21 June, and the day lasts about 8 hours instead of 16.

Sources:
NASA Space Place, What Causes the Seasons?: https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/seasons/en/
NASA NSSDCA Earth Fact Sheet: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html
NASA DSCOVR EPIC images: https://epic.gsfc.nasa.gov/

Distances, day length and Sun height computed with astronomy-engine (50°N, 15°E). Images: NASA / NOAA (DSCOVR EPIC, 21 June and 21 December 2025). Narration uses a synthetic voice.
