**30 on Earth, barely 1 on Saturn 🪐**

A year is one trip around the Sun, and every planet takes a different time. At 30 Earth years you would be about 124 on Mercury, 16 on Mars and just 1 on Saturn, and you would only reach your first Neptune birthday at 164.
