### Post copy

**You weigh less at the equator ⚖️🌍**

Earth's surface acceleration is 9.780 m/s² at the equator and 9.832 m/s² at the poles. A spring scale calibrated to read 70 kg at a pole would read about 69.6 kg at the equator, about 370 g (half a percent) less, although your mass is the same. About two thirds of the difference comes from Earth's spin (about 1,670 km/h at the equator) and the rest from Earth's squashed shape: the equator is about 21 km farther from the centre.

Source:
NASA NSSDCA Earth Fact Sheet: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html

Images: NASA / JSC (Apollo 17), NASA / NOAA (DSCOVR EPIC). Narration uses a synthetic voice.
