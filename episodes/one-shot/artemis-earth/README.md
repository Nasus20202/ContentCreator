**Earth, photographed from behind the Moon 🌍🌘**

On 6 April 2026, the Artemis II crew looked back from behind the Moon. The Moon's far side fills the foreground; the tiny crescent beyond it is Earth, about 384,400 km away on average. Both are lit by the same Sun, which is why they show different phases from where the crew was.
