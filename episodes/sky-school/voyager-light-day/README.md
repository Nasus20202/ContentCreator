### Post copy

**Voyager 1 is now a whole light-day from Earth 🛰️**

On 18 November 2026 at 10:16 UTC (11:16 CET), Voyager 1 becomes the first human-made object one light-day from Earth: about 26 billion km, the distance light travels in 24 hours. A command from Earth now takes a full day to reach it, and the reply another day to come back. It took 49 years, two months and 11 days to get there.

Source:
NASA Science, Voyager 1: What Is a Light-Day: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/voyager-1/voyager-1-what-is-a-light-day/
