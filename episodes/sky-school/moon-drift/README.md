### Post copy

**The Moon is drifting away as fast as your fingernails grow 🌕**

The Moon moves about 3.8 cm farther from Earth every year, roughly the rate your fingernails grow. We know because Apollo astronauts left mirrors on the Moon: a laser fired at them comes back in about 2.5 seconds, pinning the distance down to a few millimetres. Since Apollo 14's reflector was set up in 1971, that adds up to about 2 metres. One day the Moon will look too small to cover the Sun, ending total solar eclipses, but not for another 600 million years or more.

Sources:
NASA, Laser Beams Reflected Between Earth and Moon Boost Science: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/laser-beams-reflected-between-earth-and-moon-boost-science/
NASA Science, Tidal Locking: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/tidal-locking/
NASA Science, Eclipses and the Moon: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/eclipses/
